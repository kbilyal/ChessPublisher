Good to know

Support is always optional.

Does contributing unlock features?

No. Chess-Publisher remains free and open-source. Contributions do not unlock features or change access to the application.

Where is the payment processed?

The contribution is completed on the official PayPal.Me page at paypal.me/KBILYAL. The Chess-Publisher website does not process or store payment-card or bank-account details.

Can I still report bugs without contributing?

Yes. Bug reports and organizer feedback are welcome regardless of whether you make a contribution.
